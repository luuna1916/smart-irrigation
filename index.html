Recommendations use simulated sensor readings, forecast rain, soil properties, and plant targets.

Assumptions & formula

  1. Moisture is volumetric water content (%) from a capacitive sensor. It does not measure nutrients.
  2. Deficit (mm) = (target % - reading %) / 100 x root depth (mm). 1 mm over 1 mÂ² = 1 L.
  3. Effective forecast rain (mm) = max(0, forecast - 2) x 0.8. Skip if it covers 80% or more of the deficit.
  4. Litres = (deficit - effective rain) x tray area / boundary efficiency (0.90, assumed). Cycle and daily limits apply.
  5. Soil and plant values are estimates for this demo, not lab-calibrated values.
